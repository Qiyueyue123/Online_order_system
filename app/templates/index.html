Authentic Japanese matcha, whisked fresh for pickup.

Hello! We are Qiyue and Yuxun from Singapore, and we are making fresh Japanese matcha lattes from 8 June to 16 June 2026. There is no fixed timing: if we are in, message us and we will help you make one.

Whisking matcha is usually done with water. If you choose oat for whisking, it tastes creamier but may leave a little powder sediment.

We whisk 1g matcha with 10ml water or oat, depending on your preference. The milk or oat base is the bulk of the drink: the standard drink has 4g matcha, 120ml milk or oat base, and 4g agave syrup.
