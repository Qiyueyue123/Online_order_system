Before ordering

Sale info

A short pickup-only matcha run from Haarweg 333 Block C 053. Drinks are whisked fresh, served cold with store-bought ice, and ready at your selected pickup time.

Pickup

Orders are made for the pickup time selected at checkout. Please message us when you arrive at Haarweg 333 Block C 053.

Please allow 5-10 minutes per order. If you are running late, contact us by WhatsApp or Telegram.

Payment

You can pay cash on pickup or choose a manual Tikkie payment request. If you choose Tikkie, we will send the payment request to the WhatsApp number or Telegram handle you provide.

EUR 0.20 of each listed drink price covers the plastic cup and lid. This explains part of the pricing and is not a separate deposit or refundable charge.

Do not pay any payment link unless it comes directly from our listed contact.

Cancellation

After ordering, you receive a private management link. Anyone with that link can view and cancel the order before the pickup time.

If you already paid, or if the pickup time has passed, message us directly so we can handle it manually.

Allergens

All drinks use regular full cream milk as the base. The frothier style also uses oat milk during whisking.

Drinks contain matcha and therefore caffeine. The drinks are prepared in a dorm kitchen, so traces of milk, oat, and other kitchen ingredients may be present.

If you have a serious allergy, please do not order unless you have checked with us first.

Recipe

We use a base of 30ml regular full cream milk per 1g of matcha. The standard drink is 4g matcha with 120ml milk and 4g agave syrup.

At checkout, you can set the exact extra agave syrup in grams and adjust the regular milk volume in millilitres.

Privacy

We collect your name, WhatsApp number or Telegram handle, order items, pickup time, payment method, and optional notes only to prepare and manage your order.

Only the cafe admins can see order details. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. If you want your order details deleted after the sale, contact us.

Food Safety

Drinks are made in small batches for same-day pickup using store-bought ice cubes. Please collect close to your selected time and drink soon after pickup.

This is a small short-run sale, not a full restaurant service. If the sale grows, we will need a more formal food-safety and business setup.
