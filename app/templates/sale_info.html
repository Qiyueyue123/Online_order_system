Before ordering

Sale info

A short pickup-only matcha run from 8 June to 16 June 2026 at Haarweg 333 Block C 053. Drinks are whisked fresh, served cold with store-bought ice, and confirmed manually through WhatsApp or Telegram.

Pickup

There is no fixed timing. Message us through WhatsApp or Telegram; if we are in, we will help you make one.

Please allow 5-10 minutes per order after we confirm. If plans change, message us in the same chat.

Payment

You can pay cash on pickup or request a Tikkie link. If you choose Tikkie, we will send it in the same chat after confirming your order.

If you pay cash, please bring the exact amount because we will not have change.

EUR 0.20 of each listed drink price covers the plastic cup and lid. This explains part of the pricing and is not a separate deposit or refundable charge.

Do not pay any payment link unless it comes directly from our listed contact.

Cancellation

Rescheduling is allowed. Message us as early as possible if you want to change your pickup time.

Cancellations are not possible after payment because we plan ingredients and preparation around confirmed orders.

Allergens

The milk or oat base is the bulk of the drink. A standard drink uses 120ml milk or oat as the base.

The 10ml water or oat used for whisking matcha is separate from that base.

Drinks contain matcha and therefore caffeine. The drinks are prepared in a dorm kitchen, so traces of milk, oat, and other kitchen ingredients may be present.

If you have a serious allergy, please do not order unless you have checked with us first.

Recipe

Whisking matcha is usually done with water. If you choose oat for whisking, it tastes creamier but may leave a little powder sediment.

We whisk 1g matcha with 10ml water or oat, depending on your preference. The standard drink is 4g matcha with 120ml milk or oat base and 4g agave syrup.

When ordering, you can ask for less or extra syrup, and milk or oat base adjustments up to plus or minus 30ml in 10ml chunks.

Privacy

For manual orders, we only use your WhatsApp or Telegram message to confirm the drink, pickup time, and payment preference.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. If you want your order messages deleted after the sale, contact us in the same chat.

Food Safety

Drinks are made in small batches for same-day pickup using store-bought ice cubes. Please collect close to your selected time and drink soon after pickup.

This is a small short-run sale, not a full restaurant service. If the sale grows, we will need a more formal food-safety and business setup.
